1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you express an interest in obtaining information about us or our products and Services, or when you contact us. We also automatically collect certain information when you visit our site, such as your IP address, browser type, and device information. We also use cookies and similar tracking technologies — including the Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Pixel and the TikTok Pixel — to understand how you browse and shop on our site, for analytics and advertising purposes (see Sections 4 and 8).

2. HOW DO WE PROCESS YOUR INFORMATION?

We process your information to provide, improve, and administer our Services, communicate with you, to market and advertise our products and measure the performance of our advertising, for security and fraud prevention, and to comply with law. We may also process your information for other purposes only with your prior explicit consent.

3. WHAT LEGAL BASES DO WE RELY ON?

We only process your personal information when we believe it is necessary and we have a valid legal reason to do so — with your consent, to comply with laws, to provide you with services, to protect your rights, or to fulfill our legitimate business interests. If you are located in the EU, UK, or Canada, additional rights apply to you under GDPR and applicable law.

4. WHEN AND WITH WHOM DO WE SHARE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION?

We may share your information with third-party vendors and service providers that perform services for us, including Shopify (our e-commerce platform, checkout, and payment processing), Printify (product fulfillment and shipping), and Etsy (where we also list our products). For advertising and measurement, we share certain activity data — such as pages viewed, items added to cart, and purchases — with advertising partners including Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram) via the Meta Pixel and Conversions API, and TikTok via the TikTok Pixel, so we can measure ad performance and show you relevant ads. This sharing for targeted advertising may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under certain US state privacy laws. You can opt out by adjusting your browser cookie settings, your Meta Off-Facebook Activity settings, or by contacting us at nullmade.shop@gmail.com. We may also share your information in connection with a business transfer. We do not otherwise sell your personal information.
